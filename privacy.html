Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

What we collect

Canvas Era does not collect personal data. We do not have user accounts, comments, or newsletters.

We use Google Analytics (gtag.js G-5RYTLTMEKT) only to understand anonymous traffic (which pages are viewed). Google may use cookies.

You can opt-out via Google Analytics Opt-out.

Cookies, Local Storage & Advertising

We use:

  • Cookies: For Google Analytics and cookie consent banner.
  • Advertising Cookies: We use third-party advertising partners such as Adsterra to keep Canvas Era free. These partners may use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies to serve relevant ads based on your visits to our site and other sites.
  • LocalStorage: To save your "Saved Artworks" locally in your own browser. We never send this to our server. Clearing your browser data will delete saved items.

We use only non-intrusive ad formats (Native Banners). We do NOT use popups, popunders, or aggressive redirects.

You can control cookies in your browser settings and you can opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting YourOnlineChoices or AboutAds Opt-out.

Third-Party Partners

Our advertising partner Adsterra may collect anonymous data to improve ad relevance. Please review Adsterra's Privacy Policy.

External Links

We link to Ko-fi and Buy Me a Coffee for voluntary support. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
