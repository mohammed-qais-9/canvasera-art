Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

What we collect

Canvas Era does not collect personal data. We do not have user accounts, comments, or newsletters.

We use Google Analytics (gtag.js G-5RYTLTMEKT) only to understand anonymous traffic (which pages are viewed). Google may use cookies.

You can opt-out via Google Analytics Opt-out.

Cookies, Local Storage & Advertising

We use:

  • Cookies: For Google Analytics and cookie consent banner.
  • LocalStorage: To save your "Saved Artworks" locally in your own browser. We never send this to our server. Clearing your browser data will delete saved items.
  • Future Advertising: We may use non-intrusive advertising partners in the future to keep Canvas Era running. Currently, no ads are active. When ads are enabled, partners may use cookies to serve relevant ads.

You can control cookies in your browser settings and you can opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting YourOnlineChoices or AboutAds Opt-out.

External Links

We link to our community and social profiles: Telegram @canvasera, Pinterest, Tumblr, and Threads. Those platforms have their own privacy policies.

Telegram Community
