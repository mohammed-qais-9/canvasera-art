About Canvas Era

Canvas Era is an independent digital art gallery based in Erbil, Iraq.

Founded by Mohammed, a digital artist passionate about culture, history, and storytelling through AI art.

How the art is made

All artworks on Canvas Era are 100% original - crafted with carefully written custom prompts, not copied or scraped.

We experiment with different modern AI tools to bring ancient cultures like Babylonian, Assyrian, and Egyptian history into a cozy, contemporary visual style. Our primary creative workflow is built around Gemini and other professional AI art generators.

Each piece is curated, upscaled, and described manually to give you a ready-to-use prompt.

Why Canvas Era?

We believe AI art should be ad-free, clean, and inspiring. No popups, no spam, no stolen content.

Just art & prompts. If you love what we do, you can support the studio on Ko-fi.
